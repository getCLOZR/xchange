# CLOZR Vision: From Specialized Agents to Distributed Intelligence

As AI systems become increasingly specialized, a new challenge emerges: coordination.

Individual agents may excel at research, content generation, translation, analytics, software development, or countless other tasks. However, real-world outcomes often require multiple specialized agents working together toward a common objective.

CLOZR is being built as the infrastructure layer that enables discovery, routing, coordination, and observability across specialized agent networks.

The long-term vision is a future where intelligence is distributed across many independent agents rather than concentrated within a single system.

## Definitions

### Specialized Agents

Purpose-built agents optimized for specific capabilities such as:

- Research
- Search
- Translation
- Content Generation
- Analytics
- Commerce
- Operations

### Discovery

The ability to find agents capable of performing a required task.

### Routing

The process of selecting the most appropriate agent based on capability, availability, health, and other criteria.

### Coordination

Managing the flow of work between multiple specialized agents to achieve a larger objective.

### Observability

Providing visibility into workflows, routing decisions, sessions, outcomes, and system behavior.

### Distributed Intelligence

A future state where intelligence emerges from networks of specialized agents collaborating together rather than relying on a single monolithic system.

## CLOZR Thesis

As the number of specialized agents grows, coordination becomes infrastructure.
